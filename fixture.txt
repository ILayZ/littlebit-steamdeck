One day a little girl found a red ball in the garden. She picked it up and ran to show her brother. He smiled and asked if they could play together.
The rain came down all morning. Ben sat by the window with his toy boat. When the sun came out, his dad took him outside to float it in a puddle.
A small bird landed on the fence. Lily stood very still. The bird looked at her, shook its wings and flew back to its nest in the tall tree.
Tom wanted to bake a cake for his mum. He put flour in a bowl and cracked an egg. Some of the flour fell on his nose and made his sister laugh.
The dog could hear a soft sound under the bed. He looked down and saw a kitten hiding behind a shoe. He lay on the floor until she came out.
Sam had two apples in his bag. His friend had forgotten her lunch, so he gave her the bigger apple. They sat under a tree and ate together.
Mia heard thunder and pulled her blanket over her head. Her father came into the room with a book. They read until the storm passed.
Jack planted a seed in a small pot. Every morning he gave it a little water. One day he saw a green leaf and called his mother to look.
